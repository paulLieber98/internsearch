Project focused on automatically finding eligible internship listings as a first year data science major.

## what makes an internship "eligible" in this case ?
- available to current first-years 
- datascience/ML/related role
- must be an internship
- summer 2027
- in the US

## why make this project ?
- as a first-year, almost no internship listing applies to me because of my graduation year
- i find it annoying to have to click through every listing just to be met with "must be pursuing a MS or Phd" or even 
a "must graduate between the years 2027-2028", etc....
- regardless of what it is, this process is tedious. i don't want to do it myself.

## how does internsearch work ?
- pulls from different job search engines w/ python
- store listings in postgresql
- filter through listings
- return eligible listings
